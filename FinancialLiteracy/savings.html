Three practical habits

  1. Save regularly. Set aside a small, realistic amount when you receive money, after covering essential expenses.
  2. Prepare for surprises. Build a separate emergency reserve gradually; even a small start can help.
  3. Pause before non-essential purchases. Waiting a day or two can help you decide whether you still want the item and can afford it.

Choose a safe, accessible place for your savings and understand any fees or withdrawal rules. This lesson is educational, not financial advice.
