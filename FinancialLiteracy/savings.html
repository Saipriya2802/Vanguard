Core Financial Law

Rule Spotlight: "Pay Yourself First"

Most students wait until the end of the month to save whatever cash is left over — which is usually ₹0. The golden habit: The moment your allowance or gift money enters your hands, immediately route a fixed 20% into your Emergency Jar, then spend the rest guilt-free!

Stage 3 of 4 • Where to Keep Savings

Safe vs. Unsafe Tools: Where Does Your Money Live?

Not all savings places are created equal. Compare beginner-friendly storage vehicles to protect your cash from theft, inflation, and impulse leaks!

❌ High Risk
📦💵

Under the Mattress / Cash Box

Keeping paper currency notes in your study desk, cupboard, or hostel box.

Theft / Loss Risk: Very High (Zero Protection)
Growth / Interest: 0% (Eaten by inflation)
Impulse Leak: Tempting (Too easy to spend)
🛡️ Safe & Regulated
🏦💳

Zero-Balance Savings Account

RBI-regulated bank account (PMJDY style) with zero minimum balance fees.

Security: Insured up to ₹5 Lakh
Growth / Interest: 2.5% – 3.0% Annual
Access: Secure UPI & Debit Card
⭐ Habit Champion
🔒📈

Recurring Deposit (RD)

Automated monthly lock (₹200–₹500/month) for 6 to 12 months with guaranteed interest.

Discipline: Automated Savings Habit
Guaranteed Growth: 6.5% – 7.2% Fixed Returns
Impulse Protection: Locked away from cravings!
