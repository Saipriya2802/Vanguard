⚡ Quick Rule of Thumb: The 50 / 30 / 20 Guideline

The golden framework to prevent running out of money before month-end.

See breakdown for:
50% Needs (₹1,500)
30% Wants (₹900)
20% Savings (₹600)
50% Needs (Survival & Study) Non-Negotiable Essentials

TSRTC bus pass, college metro smart card, essential mess food, lab manual xerox, monthly mobile study data.

30% Wants (Fun & Lifestyle) Comforts & Fun

Sandhya RTC X Roads movies, canteen biryani treats, trendy sneakers, online gaming skins, bubble tea.

20% Savings (Future Shield) Emergency & Goals

Entrance exam application fee, emergency health buffer, saving up for college backpack or laptop.
