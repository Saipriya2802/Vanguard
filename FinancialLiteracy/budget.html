What this game helps you notice

  • Goals make choices concrete: A target and deadline help you see whether your monthly plan is on track.
  • Compounding takes time: Growth can build on earlier growth, but the assumed rate is not guaranteed.
  • Diversification spreads risk: Different buckets can behave differently; diversification cannot remove every risk.
  • Compare and replay: A safe-only plan may grow too slowly, while a volatile plan can lose value. Try another allocation and compare.
