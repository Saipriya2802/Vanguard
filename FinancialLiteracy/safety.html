Feature 2 • Protect Your Money

Keep Your UPI PINs, OTPs, and Banking Details Safe

Learn the non-negotiable rules of digital banking safety through an authentic real-life simulation.

🔐 Golden Rule 1 UPI PIN is ONLY for SENDING Money

You NEVER enter your UPI PIN to receive money or get a refund. Entering a PIN always DEBITS your account!

🔐 Golden Rule 2 OTPs are Strictly Private

No legitimate bank officer, police, or courier company will ever ask for your bank OTP. Anyone asking for OTP is a scammer!

🔐 Golden Rule 3 Beware Screen Sharing Apps

Never install apps like AnyDesk, RustDesk, or TeamViewer on requests from unknown callers. They view your screen and steal OTPs!
