Stay Financially Safe

Pause, verify, and protect your personal and payment information.

🔐

Keep OTPs and PINs private

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, password, or card security code—even with someone claiming to be from a bank.

🔎

Verify before you click or pay

Avoid unexpected links and QR codes. Open the official app or website yourself and confirm payment requests independently.

⏸️

Pause when someone pressures you

Urgent threats, prize claims, and requests to install screen-sharing apps are warning signs. Stop and speak to someone you trust.

🛡️

If you lose money to a cyber scam

Contact your bank using its official number and report financial cyber fraud promptly by calling India’s helpline 1930 or visiting cybercrime.gov.in.
