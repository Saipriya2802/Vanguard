Understand Investing

Learn how time, compounding, and risk can affect investments.

⏳

Give investments time

Investments can rise and fall. Money you may need soon is usually better kept somewhere stable and easy to access.

🌱

Understand compounding

Compounding happens when returns can earn returns too. Over time, this may help money grow, but growth is never guaranteed.

🧺

Spread risk

Diversification means spreading money across different investments instead of relying on just one. It can reduce some risks, not remove them all.
