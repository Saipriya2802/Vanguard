Feature 3 • Risk vs. Return Challenge

Compare Two Investments: Choose Based on Situation

Higher potential return always demands higher risk tolerance. Match the right investment tool to each real-life student scenario!

🛡️ Low Risk ~6.5% Annual Return

Option A: Bank Fixed Deposit / Govt Bond

Guaranteed capital safety. Principal never drops, regardless of market crashes.

Volatility: Zero (Stable)
Best For: Short-term goals (0–12 months)
⚡ High Risk ~12% – 15% Annual Return

Option B: Equity Index / Tech Stocks

Substantial long-term wealth growth, but experiences short-term swings of ±15% to ±25%.

Volatility: High (Swings up & down)
Best For: Long-term goals (5+ years)
